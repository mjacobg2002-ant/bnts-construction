About BNTS Construction

Two Decades In The Ground, One Standard Above It.

Founder Bomy Souksengmany has spent over 23 years in the construction industry, specializing in foundation and structural concrete work across Northern Virginia.

Established in 2019, BNTS Construction LLC was built around a single goal: to deliver high-quality, code-compliant work that homeowners and builders can rely on long-term. From footings and foundation walls to luxury stamped patios, every project is held to the same structural standard.
